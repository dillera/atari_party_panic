Bunker
This hidden bunker is a computing time capsule. Racks of 1970s and 80s computer equipment line the walls, status lights blinking back on after all these years. The air has a distinct ozone smell mixed with old electronics.

Emergency lighting bathes everything in an eerie blue glow. Most of the equipment has gone dark, except for the central Atari system which runs on its own power supply.
In the center of the room stands a heavy metal table, supporting a heavily modified Atari 800 connected to what appears to be a military-grade telecom system with an old modem. A technical notebook rests beside the computer as well
